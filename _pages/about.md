I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Geography & Geographic Information Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Prof. Shaowen Wang. I received my Ph.D. in Geo-information Science and Earth Observation from the University of Twente in 2025.

My research uses geospatial data science and AI to characterize flood and dam-failure hazards and the buildings and communities exposed to them. I combine observation-based flood modeling and dam-breach inundation modeling with building-use and neighborhood information to support exposure assessment and hazard preparedness.

## Research Interests

- **Dam-breach hazards and preparedness**: Physics-guided deep learning surrogates for dam-breach inundation mapping
- **Observation-based flood-depth prediction**: Predicting flood peak depth from USGS high-water marks, terrain, Earth observation embeddings, and meteorological forcing
- **Building use and urban functional zones**: Mapping building use, mixed use, and urban functional zones from imagery and geospatial text
- **Urban livability evaluation**: Multi-task multimodal deep learning for neighborhood conditions and livability
- **Geospatial foundation models**: Evaluating foundation-model embeddings where socioeconomic data are scarce
- **Multimodal geospatial data fusion**: Combining remote sensing, GIS, text, and time-series data
