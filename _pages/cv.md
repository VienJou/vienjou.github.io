Research Profile
======
My research uses geospatial data science and AI to characterize flood and dam-failure hazards and the buildings and communities exposed to them. I combine observation-based flood modeling and dam-breach inundation modeling with building-use and neighborhood information to support exposure assessment and hazard preparedness. My developing research program examines how uncertainty in these data affects assessments of risk and decisions about mitigation and preparedness.

Datasets and Software
======
* **Multimodal Urban Livability Evaluation Dataset and Model.** About 52,000 samples from urban areas in the Netherlands combining remote sensing imagery, digital surface models, night-light imagery, and point-of-interest text, with labels for an overall livability score and five domain scores; released with a pretrained model on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Vinjou/Multimodal_urban_livability_evaluation_dataset) (CC BY-NC 4.0). Accompanies Zhou et al., Remote Sensing of Environment (2026).
* **Doctoral Research Data and Code.** Data and code supporting four doctoral studies on building-use classification and urban livability, archived at DANS ([DOI 10.17026/PT/VFTW9X](https://doi.org/10.17026/PT/VFTW9X)).
* **USGS High-Water-Mark Flood Peak Dataset.** Benchmark pairing USGS high-water-mark surveys from 39 U.S. flood events with terrain, Earth observation embeddings, and meteorological forcing; under construction, with public release planned on completion.
